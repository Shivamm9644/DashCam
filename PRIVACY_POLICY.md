# Privacy Policy for TrackAnywhere

**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

Welcome to **TrackAnywhere** ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related fleet telematics services.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Location Information:** We collect precise and approximate GPS location data from vehicles and dashcam devices to enable live fleet tracking, route replay, trip logs, and geofencing alerts.
* **Camera & Video Data:** TrackAnywhere processes live video streams and recorded video segments (Road Camera and Cabin Camera) transmitted from connected DashCam hardware for telematics, safety monitoring, and incident investigation.
* **Account Information:** To provide role-based access, we store user credentials including username, email address, role, and tenant association.
* **Device Telematics Data:** We collect device status metrics such as vehicle speed, ignition status, satellite fix quality, and SOS alarm triggers.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

* To provide real-time GPS tracking and live DashCam streaming.
* To generate trip histories, safety logs, and emergency collision/SOS notifications.
* To administer multi-tenant fleet accounts and enforce role-based access controls.
* To maintain service security, diagnose technical issues, and improve platform performance.

---

### 3. Data Protection and Security

* **Encryption in Transit:** All communications between the mobile application, cloud servers, and DashCam devices are transmitted securely using industry-standard HTTPS, WSS, and TLS protocols.
* **Access Control:** User access is protected via cryptographically signed JWT tokens and strict tenant isolation.
* **No Third-Party Advertising:** TrackAnywhere does not display advertisements, sell your data, or share personal information with third-party advertising networks.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide telematics services or as required by commercial fleet agreements. Users may request the deletion of their personal account and associated data at any time by following the instructions at:  
[Data Deletion Instructions](https://github.com/Shivamm9644/DashCam/blob/main/DATA_DELETION.md)
