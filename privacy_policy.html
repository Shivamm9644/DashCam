Privacy Policy for TrackAnywhere

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Application: TrackAnywhere (Package: com.trackanywhere.dashcam)

Welcome to TrackAnywhere ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of fleet operators, drivers, and users. This Privacy Policy details the information we collect, how it is used, and the robust safeguards in place to protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Precise & Approximate Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates, vehicle speed, direction, and route logs collected from fleet telematics hardware and dashcams to provide live vehicle tracking, trip history, geofencing, and route replay.
  • Dashcam Video & Audio Feeds: Live telematics video streaming and incident recordings (Road & Cabin cameras) transmitted by authorized connected DashCam hardware for driver safety, collision investigation, and security verification.
  • Account & User Information: Username, email address, assigned roles, company/fleet affiliation, and encrypted authentication tokens.
  • Device Diagnostics & Telematics: Terminal IDs, vehicle license plates, ignition status, hardware health indicators, network signal strength, and emergency SOS alerts.

2. How We Use Collected Data

  • To stream real-time DashCam video and display live vehicle locations on maps.
  • To calculate trip analytics, driver performance statistics, and route replays.
  • To dispatch instant collision, speeding, and geofence breach notifications.
  • To authenticate users and enforce organizational multi-tenant security boundaries.
  • Zero Ad Targeting: We do NOT sell personal data or license it to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection and Security

Security Protocols: All communication between client applications, backend gateways, and dashcam terminals is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS and WebSockets (WSS). Access is governed by cryptographically signed JWT authentication tokens.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users have the right to access, inspect, or request permanent deletion of their personal data and account credentials at any time. For comprehensive account deletion procedures, please visit our Account and Data Deletion Policy or contact our data protection team.
