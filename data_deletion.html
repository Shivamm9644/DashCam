At TrackAnywhere, we provide our users full control over their account data in compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies.

How to Request Account & Personal Data Deletion

Step 1: Submit Your Request via Email
Send an email to our support administration team at:
shivam.inurum@gmail.com

Subject Line: Delete Account Request - TrackAnywhere

Step 2: Required Account Information
Please include:

  • Your registered Username / Email Address
  • Your Organization or Fleet Tenant Name (if applicable)

Step 3: Verification & Execution
Our administration verifies user requests within 48 hours. Upon verification:

  • Your user account, personal profile, email, and authentication keys are permanently purged within 7 business days.
  • A final confirmation notification is sent to your email upon completion.

Data Retention vs. Permanent Deletion

  • Permanently Deleted: User login credentials, user personal profile, mobile notification tokens, and individual app preferences.
  • Retained Operational Records: Aggregated fleet telematics logs (vehicle GPS records, telematics telemetry) may be retained by commercial enterprise administrators strictly as required by local transport regulations and enterprise service level agreements.
