# Account and Data Deletion Policy for TrackAnywhere

**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

At **TrackAnywhere**, we respect your right to control your personal data. This document outlines how users can request the deletion of their user account and associated personal data.

---

### How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users can request permanent deletion of their account by following these simple steps:

1. **Send an Email Request:**  
   Send an email to our support team at:  
   **[shivam.inurum@gmail.com](mailto:shivam.inurum@gmail.com)**  
   * **Subject:** `Delete Account Request - TrackAnywhere`

2. **Provide Verification Details:**  
   In your email, please provide:
   * Your registered username / email address
   * Your Company / Tenant name (if applicable)

3. **Processing & Confirmation:**  
   * Our support team will verify your identity within 48 hours.
   * Upon verification, your user profile, login credentials, and session tokens will be permanently deleted from our databases within **7 business days**.
   * A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion is complete.

---

### Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

* **Deleted Data:**
  * User profile information (username, email, password hash, role).
  * Active session tokens and authorization records.
* **Data Retention:**
  * Fleet telematics data (GPS trip logs, dashcam incident clips) associated with commercial fleet operations may be retained by your fleet administrator according to statutory commercial records regulations or organizational agreements.
